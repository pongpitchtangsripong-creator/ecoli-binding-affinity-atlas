Reversible inhibitor • experimental ribosome pose

Chloramphenicol + 50S ribosomal subunit

CAM occupies the A-site cleft of the peptidyl-transferase centre in 23S rRNA and obstructs productive aminoacyl-tRNA accommodation.

Affinity
2.2 µM Kd; independent historical estimates are ~2–3 µM.
Bonding
Non-covalent: H-bonding involving A2062/G2505, nitrobenzyl stacking on C2452, van der Waals contacts, and K⁺-mediated coordination in the pocket.
Structure
PDB 4V7T, E. coli 70S–chloramphenicol crystal structure; this view extracts one 50S particle and CLM.

Confirmed structure + literature-supported equilibrium affinity

02

Native substrate • functional 70S context

50S catalytic centre + A/P-site tRNAs

The 50S subunit catalyses peptide-bond formation between aminoacyl-tRNA in the A site and peptidyl-tRNA in the P site. tRNA binding spans both ribosomal subunits, so isolated-50S and programmed-70S values are not interchangeable.

Affinity
~0.0083–0.25 µM Kd, calculated from published Ka values of 0.4–12 × 10⁷ M⁻¹ for defined A/P/E-site ligands at 15 mM Mg²⁺.
Bonding
Extensive RNA–RNA recognition, codon–anticodon pairing on 30S, Mg²⁺-stabilized electrostatics, and 3′-CCA/end-group placement in the 50S PTC.
Structure
PDB 8G6Y, E. coli ribosome with A- and P-site tRNAs. The A-site monomer is non-natural; the tRNA/ribosome geometry is experimental.

Confirmed structure; affinity is a context-dependent range from programmed 70S assays

03

Time-dependent β-lactam inhibitor

Cefuroxime + PBP3 (FtsI)

CEF first forms a recognition complex, then its β-lactam carbonyl is attacked by catalytic Ser307 to form a long-lived acyl-enzyme and block septal transpeptidation.

Potency
0.02 ± 0.01 µM IC50,app in EDTA-permeabilized E. coli MG1655; 0.04 µg/mL (~0.094 µM) in E. coli DC2 independently supports PBP3 selectivity.
Bonding
Covalent acyl bond to Ser307 after ring opening; the oxyanion hole is defined by backbone nitrogens of Ser307 and Thr497, with Lys310/Ser359/Asn361/Lys494/Thr495/Gly496/Thr497 forming the catalytic environment.
Structure
E. coli PBP3 template PDB 4BJP with a locally generated geometric CEF pose. The viewer is not an experimental cefuroxime–EcPBP3 crystal structure and does not encode the acyl bond.

Potency confirmed; displayed ligand pose is computational

04

Native enzymatic substrate • affinity gap disclosed

PBP3 + peptidoglycan stem pentapeptide

The donor substrate is L-Ala–γ-D-Glu–mDAP–D-Ala–D-Ala. Ser307 forms an acyl-enzyme with the penultimate D-Ala, the terminal D-Ala leaves, and the mDAP amine of an acceptor stem resolves the intermediate to create the cross-link.

Affinity
N.D. A defensible direct Kd for native E. coli PBP3–stem-peptide binding was not located. Catalytic substrate recognition cannot be replaced safely by a β-lactam IC50.
Bonding
Transient covalent acyl-enzyme at Ser307; Lys310 promotes acylation, Ser359/Lys494 assist catalysis, Asn361 positions the interpeptide group, and Thr495/Thr497 anchor the C-terminal carboxylate.
Structure
PDB 4BJP apo E. coli PBP3. Green beads illustrate the proposed substrate path only; they are not atomistic docking coordinates.

Mechanism supported; direct native-substrate affinity not determined
